Criteria for successful Savings Account Opening Application
Here are 3 simple eligibility rules for a Savings Account Opening Application:
- Applicant must be at least 18 years old and provide a valid government-issued photo ID.
- Applicant must submit valid proof of address and complete KYC verification requirements.
- Applicant must make an initial deposit of at least £100 to activate the savings account.
Alternative version with salary criteria:
- Applicant must be at least 18 years old and have a valid photo ID.
- Applicant must have a minimum monthly income of £1,500.
- Applicant must pass KYC verification and make an opening deposit of at least £100.


Demat Account Rules
- Applicant must be at least 18 years old and complete KYC verification with valid PAN and photo ID.
- Applicant must provide a valid bank account linked to the Demat account.
- Applicant must successfully complete identity and address verification before account activation.


Current Account Rules
- Applicant must provide valid business or personal identity documents and complete KYC requirements.
- Applicant must maintain a minimum opening deposit of £500 (or equivalent local currency).
- Applicant must have no history of fraudulent banking activities or account restrictions.


Fixed Deposit (FD) Rules
- Applicant must hold an active savings or current account with the bank.
- Minimum fixed deposit amount must be £1,000 (or equivalent local currency).
- Deposit funds must remain locked in for the selected tenure unless premature withdrawal conditions are met.
