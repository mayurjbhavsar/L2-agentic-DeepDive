**Loan Application Risk Assessment

Build an Al-assisted loan application review service that summarizes applicant
information, identifies missing documents, highlights potential risk factors, and generates
an explainable recommendation for a loan officer.

***Problem statement

A bank receives many loan applications with supporting documents such as income
statements, employment details, credit notes, and policy guidelines. Loan officers spend
significant time reading documents and checking eligibility rules. The organization wants
an internal assistant that can help officers review applications faster while keeping the final
decision under human control.
